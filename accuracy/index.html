Each morning the site stores the NWS gridpoint snow and QPF totals (24h, 72h, 7 days) at the same SNOTEL forecast points the table uses, then later compares them to NRCS SNOTEL. SWE change is the primary score (NWS QPF vs station WTEQ). Depth change is secondary — pillows settle, so a storm can add snow while the depth sensor barely moves or even drops.
